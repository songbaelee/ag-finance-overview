Evidence from the Field

Most of this site relies on illustrative modeling — estimates and segmentations built to show the shape of the market, always clearly labeled as "our illustrative modeling." This page is the exception. It presents actual figures reported by real lenders and programs, not modeled ones.

What the report tells us

How profitable is agricultural lending, and does that change with loan size? The figures below come from a market-wide benchmarking study of East African bank profitability — not from any single lender's portfolio, and not from Aceli Africa's own incentive-program data. Most of the underlying loans predate or fall outside that program entirely.

Source: Dalberg Advisors / Aceli Africa, “Learning Report: Year 3 — Unlocking Finance for African Agriculture” (2024). Read the full report

What the report also tells us: opportunity cost

Agri-SME lending doesn't happen in a vacuum — capital that goes into it could otherwise fund other things. The report's Diagram 4 gives two market-wide reference points for the same countries: what banks earn on lending generally (without agri-SME incentives), and what a government bond yields.

Source: Dalberg Advisors / Aceli Africa, “Learning Report: Year 3 — Unlocking Finance for African Agriculture” (2024), Diagram 4. Read the full report
